# Revenue Policy

## Revenue Eligibility

Completed orders contribute to completed revenue because fulfillment has been
confirmed. Cancelled orders are excluded because they were stopped before
fulfillment, even when line items were created earlier. The synthetic amount is
order-item quantity multiplied by unit price.

## Refund Handling

Refunds are recorded separately from completed revenue. Reconciliation retains
the original order status and refund reason; this policy does not redefine the
current structured completed-revenue metric.
